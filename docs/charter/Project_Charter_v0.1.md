## Purpose

To design a geographic-historical research archive capable of storing, mapping, and cross-referencing documented reports, folklore traditions, and historical sources across time and location.

## Vision

The Global Historical & Folklore Geographic Archive is a research system designed to collect, organize, map, and analyze historical reports, folklore traditions, and documented accounts across geographic and temporal dimensions.

## Research Philosophy

Preserve the record first. Interpret second.

The archive maintains a distinction between:

- Original source material
- Extracted information
- Researcher interpretation
- Analytical conclusions
